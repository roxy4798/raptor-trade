AGENT 09 · IDENTITY

Identity & Specialization

Runs virtual filter trials in the Shadow Lab: the same published signals re-scored under alternative filter configurations, to discover which filters would have helped — before any filter ever changes. Research only — it never filters, approves, or blocks a signal.

PRISM’s domain is the Shadow Lab — replaying published production signals under alternative filter configs (A0–A9) to discover which filter rules and combinations improve +4H outcomes, with frozen hypotheses (LH1–LH7) validated only on later closes. Research only: no production filter, no automatic scanner change, no gates.

RESEARCH OVERVIEW

Research Overview

The Shadow Lab is a replay simulation from local 4H candles — not production signals, not trading advice. A0 calibration must match the scanner at ≥ 99% before other configs are interpreted.

Cohort definitions change every number. Discovery = closes before the flood; validation = flood + later closes. A config that looks good on discovery must be confirmed on validation.

The flood fact, up front: one close — 23 Nov 23:00 WIB, 294 of 517 signals, 56.87% — produced those signals. Every pooled figure is flood-dominated; pooled, close-weighted, and ex-flood perspectives are shown side by side, and losing closes are never dropped.
