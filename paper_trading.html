07 — Costs & performance

Fees, Funding & Stats

Costs use the public Binance VIP 0 schedule (taker 0.05%). Funding accrues from the mark-price stream at each contract's own settlement boundary (typically every 8h, per contract — never assumed).

PnL analytics

Price change, gross ROI, and net ROI are three different numbers: a TP close is +2% price, +40% gross ROI at 20x, and a lower net ROI after entry/exit fees and funding. Unrealized PnL is floating (mark-to-market), not yet realized.

Fees & funding breakdown

Totals across all closed trades and open positions. A negative funding total means the virtual account paid funding; a positive total means it received funding.
