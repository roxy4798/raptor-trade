04 / STRATEGY LOGIC

Active Filters RULES 4H v2.1.2

Gates — all must pass
  • 4H candle closed — evaluation only at the close, never intra-candle.
  • Breakout: close above the previous 20-bar high, and close above this bar's open.
  • Volume: this bar's volume at least 1.3x the 60-bar median (RVOL ≥ 1.3x).
  • Dedup: one signal per symbol per 3 closes (12 hours) — once only, the fresh one.
  • Excluded symbols: USDCUSDT + USDBRLUSDT never signal — fiat-anchored instruments (stablecoin / forex rate), not pump targets.
  • Complete data: at least 61 consecutive 4H bars with no gaps; any gap means no signal.

Historical-data note: This exported history includes USDCUSDT and USDBRLUSDT despite the exclusion rule listed above. Records are retained unchanged. Check the timing of the rule rollout before drawing conclusions.

Info only — computed & logged, never blocks
  • EMA 20 / 50 / 500, ATR 14, taker-buy, momentum HH/HL, RSI 5m / 15m / 1h / 4h, live liquidity (spread, impact, depth).
