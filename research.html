      11 — Risk & Portfolio

      Agent 12 — Risk & Portfolio Intelligence

      What a strategy risks, not just what it wins — and what happens when many LONG signals fire at once. Portfolio curves are research simulations (simple cumulative sums of per-close returns, no compounding), not real trading results. Two sizing variants are compared on the same signals: fixed-notional (1 unit per signal, exposure grows with the batch) vs fixed-capital (1 unit per close, split equally). Risk of ruin is not computed — block bootstrap needs at least 60 closes, and there are only 30 so far. Everything here is research — nothing sizes a real position or sets real leverage.

      12 — Pump Ignition

      Agent 07 — Pump Ignition & Continuation

      What the pre-entry picture looks like before a signal fires — squeeze, Bollinger Band width, ATR contraction, volume acceleration, price compression, momentum acceleration, and pre-breakout open interest — measured only on bars before the signal close, never after. The goal is not the biggest pump: it is the entry that is immediately followed by a rise, so a realistic small TP is reached more often. Every number here is descriptive — one close (the 23 Nov flood) dominates the sample, and hypotheses IH1–IH6 are frozen and awaiting prospective closes. v1.1.0 adds the first pump candle itself: pump strength (open-to-close, vs ATR, vs the contract's own history), candle structure (body, wicks, close position, previous candle, exhaustion), and six separated next-candle rates — bullish close, positive gross, positive net, TP-before-SL, continuation failure, and reversal — with pooled, close-weighted, and ex-flood views. Hypotheses PH1–PH6 are frozen alongside IH1–IH6.
